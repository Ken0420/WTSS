The Story

In 2007, ThinkSmart Software's founder, Andrew Leigh, identified that businesses running classes were wasting significant amounts of time and money because they didn't have the right software and automation.

Andrew, being an experienced software developer, set about designing software solutions to automate administrative processes and redefine how these businesses are managed. George Bentley soon joined the team. Over the next few years Andrew and George worked tirelessly to build the brand in Australia. In 2012, George took the business to the United Kingdom where he manages our operations for Europe.

The rest is history as ThinkSmart continues to develop and expand its operations.
