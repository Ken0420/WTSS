THINKSMART HUB

A customer portal and online class registration system

Help your customers effortlessly sign up for classes, make bookings, update details, make online payments, catch up on your announcements, and so much more!

Manage your personal details

  • Update your contact information
  • View/update student photos
  • Add/edit your students
  • Update payment information
  • Check and sign agreements
  • View student skill sets

    • Manage your classes & financials

  • Add student enrollments
  • Notify absences
  • View class information
  • View and pay invoices
  • Book into make-up classes
  • View customer statements

    • Customise classes information with images.
